1. Gioi thieu

1.1. Muc dich

Tai lieu nay mo ta chi tiet phan tich thiet ke cua he thong TPBank CRM Mobile - ung dung quan ly quan he khach hang danh cho nhan vien kinh doanh (Relationship Manager - RM) cua Ngan hang TMCP Tien Phong (TPBank).

He thong giup RM:

  • Quan ly thong tin khach hang 360 do
  • Theo doi va chuyen doi khach hang tiem nang (Lead)
  • Quan ly co hoi kinh doanh (Opportunity)
  • Lap ke hoach va theo doi cong viec hang ngay (Task)
  • Xu ly yeu cau ho tro khach hang (Case)
  • Su dung cong cu ho tro ban hang thong minh (Sales Support + AI)

1.2. Pham vi

Tai lieu bao gom phan tich thiet ke cho cac module sau:

STTModuleMenuMo ta
1DashboardHomeMan hinh tong hop du lieu, KPI, canh bao
2Customer 360Cus360Thong tin 360 do ve khach hang
3Lead ManagementLeadsQuan ly khach hang tiem nang
4OpportunitiesOppsQuan ly co hoi kinh doanh
5Task ManagementTasksQuan ly cong viec hang ngay
6Customer ServiceMore > CasesQuan ly yeu cau ho tro
7Sales SupportMore > Sales SupportCong cu ho tro ban hang

1.3. Doi tuong su dung

Vai troMo ta
Relationship Manager (RM)Nhan vien kinh doanh truc tiep quan ly khach hang, su dung toan bo chuc nang cua ung dung
Team LeaderQuan ly nhom RM, xem bao cao tong hop va phan bo cong viec

2. Tong quan he thong

2.1. Mo ta tong quan

TPBank CRM Mobile la ung dung di dong duoc thiet ke theo phong cach Material Design 3, toi uu cho man hinh 430px (iPhone 14 Pro Max). Ung dung su dung kien truc Single Page voi bottom navigation gom 6 muc chinh:

  • Home - Trang chu Dashboard
  • Cus360 - Customer 360
  • Leads - Quan ly KH tiem nang
  • Opps - Quan ly co hoi
  • Tasks - Quan ly cong viec
  • More - Menu mo rong (Cases, Sales Support)

Cong nghe: HTML5, TailwindCSS, Chart.js, Material Symbols. Responsive mobile-first.

2.2. Cac module chinh va moi quan he

So do lien ket giua cac module:

ModuleCo the di den
Dashboard (Home)Lead Management, Opportunities, Task Management
Customer 360Opportunities, Lead Management, Task Management, Case Management
Lead ManagementCustomer 360 (chuyen doi), Opportunities (chuyen doi)
OpportunitiesCustomer 360 (chuyen doi Account)
Task ManagementCustomer 360 (lien ket KH)
CasesCustomer 360 (lien ket KH)
Sales SupportCustomer 360 (ap dung cho KH cu the)
